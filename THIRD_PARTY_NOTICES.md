# Third-party data, content, and software

The root `LICENSE` applies to software and documentation created specifically
for RCA Atlas, except where a file or directory states different terms.

RCA Atlas also indexes or preserves material obtained from external sources,
including scholarly publications and abstracts, figures and images, website
captures, instrument metadata, observational data, operational records, and
source-code snapshots. Those materials remain subject to the copyright,
license, citation, attribution, access, and use terms of their original
publishers and providers. They are not relicensed by the RCA Atlas license.

In particular:

- Publications, abstracts, figures, and supplemental material retain the
  rights and reuse terms assigned by their authors and publishers.
- Content and data obtained from COSZO, the Ocean Observatories Initiative,
  the University of Washington Interactive Oceans program, EarthScope, and
  other identified providers retain the terms published by those providers.
- Vendored code and repository snapshots retain their upstream licenses.
  Any license file included with an upstream component controls that
  component. This includes the vendored RCA data tools under
  `src/agentic_qaqc/vendor/rca_data_tools`.
- Source URLs, citations, identifiers, and provenance records are retained in
  the corpus so downstream systems can identify and attribute each source.

Redistributors and deployments are responsible for reviewing the applicable
source-specific terms before publishing or redistributing third-party
material. A file's explicit license or source terms take precedence over this
notice.
